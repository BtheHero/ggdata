How this report is made

tools/report.js in the ggdata repository reads every GitHub Actions run of the last 7 days, its jobs and its log. Success and failure are GitHub's. Social posts come from the posting lines in the Social queue, Social resend and Social weekly logs. Files uploaded come from the upload step; newer runs also list the folders and the new files. Expected runs are worked out from each workflow's cron schedule since the workflow was added.
